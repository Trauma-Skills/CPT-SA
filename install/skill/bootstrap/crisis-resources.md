# Crisis resources

If you are in immediate danger or thinking about harming yourself, please reach out now. You don't have to be in a "bad enough" state to call — these lines are for any level of distress.

**If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number** (US/Canada 911, UK 999, EU 112).

## Talk to someone now

- **US — 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline:** call or text **988**, or chat at [988lifeline.org](https://988lifeline.org). 24/7.
- **US/Canada — Crisis Text Line:** text **HOME to 741741**. 24/7.
- **UK & Ireland — Samaritans:** call **116 123** (free, 24/7), or email jo@samaritans.org.
- **UK — Shout:** text **SHOUT to 85258**. 24/7.

## Sexual abuse / assault specific

- **US — RAINN National Sexual Assault Hotline:** **1-800-656-4673**, or chat at [rainn.org](https://www.rainn.org). 24/7, confidential.

## Anywhere in the world

- **Find A Helpline:** [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com) — enter your country for local free, confidential lines.
- **Befrienders Worldwide:** [befrienders.org](https://www.befrienders.org) — international directory of emotional-support helplines.

## Grounding, right now

If you feel flooded or detached, try **5-4-3-2-1**: name 5 things you can see, 4 you can hear, 3 you can touch, 2 you can smell, 1 you can taste. Slow your out-breath so it's longer than your in-breath. You are here, and this feeling will pass.
