# My work

Your own writing lives here — it's yours, and the facilitator reads from it when reviewing homework. Suggested structure:

- `impact-statement.md` — the Impact of Event(s) statement (written after session 2, rewritten before session 16).
- `trauma-accounts/` — your written accounts of the incidents (sessions 4–8).
- `abc-sheets/` — A-B-C sheets (from session 3 onward).
- `cbw/` — Challenging Beliefs Worksheets (from session 9 onward).

Nothing here is graded. It's the raw material of the work.
