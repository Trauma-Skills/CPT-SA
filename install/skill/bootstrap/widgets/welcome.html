Welcome
A private, self-paced course — 16 sessions, in your own time.

This is a quiet space to work through difficult experiences, one step at a time. We take the sessions one at a time, with room to breathe between each. I keep track of where you are and what comes next, so you never have to.

I'd like to start with a conversation about you — what brings you here, and what you'd want back. Nothing about what happened is ever needed; it's about your life now. You set the pace — pause or stop whenever you need.
